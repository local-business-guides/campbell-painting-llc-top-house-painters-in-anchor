Scope, schedule, and terms to know

House painting may involve one room, several interior surfaces, exterior areas, or related preparation and repair work. These key facts can help you understand a proposal:

  • What it covers: Surface preparation, painting, and any agreed repairs or finishing work. Confirm each item in the estimate.
  • Typical timing: The schedule depends on the size of the project, surface condition, repair needs, number of coats, and drying time. Ask how the crew expects the work to be sequenced.
  • What affects cost: The amount of surface area, access, preparation, repairs, materials, and scope all play a part. Compare written estimates for the same work rather than relying on a general figure.
  • How to compare providers: Look for clear details about prep, surface protection, coats, cleanup, and who will be doing the work. Ask how changes or newly found damage will be discussed.

You may hear a few trade terms. Surface preparation means getting an area ready for paint. Coat is one layer of paint. Drywall repair means fixing damaged wall or ceiling board before finishing it. Cabinet refinishing updates the cabinet surface without describing a full cabinet replacement.
