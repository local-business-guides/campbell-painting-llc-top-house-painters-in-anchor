What the job covers and how to compare it

The exact work depends on the surfaces and condition of the space. An estimate can help establish what is included, how the work is sequenced and what needs attention before painting.

Key facts to review:
- Coverage: Interior or exterior painting, drywall repair, cabinet refinishing, or a combination of these.
- Timing: Ask for an expected schedule. Project length varies with the amount of work, preparation, repairs, drying time and access to the areas.
- Cost factors: The size and condition of the surfaces, repair needs, number of areas, materials and access can affect the overall cost. Get a project-specific estimate rather than relying on a general figure.
- Crew: Confirm whether the contractor’s own employees or subcontractors will do the work.

When comparing providers, check that each estimate describes similar surfaces and preparation. Ask how drywall repairs are handled, what finish is planned and who you contact if the scope needs clarification. A well known painter in Anchorage should be willing to talk through those points without leaving you to guess.

A few terms you may hear: prep means getting a surface ready for paint; drywall repair addresses damage or imperfections in wallboard; cabinet refinishing updates the cabinet surface rather than replacing the cabinets. Ask the contractor to explain any other unfamiliar term.
